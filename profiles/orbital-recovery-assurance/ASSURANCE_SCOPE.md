# Assurance Scope

Orbital Recovery Assurance provides a defined assurance layer for recovery assessment under uncertain physical state, digital trust, authority, and post-intervention mission fitness.

## Profile-owned controls

The profile provides machine-checkable structure for:

- physical, trust, and authority state separation;
- evidence references, provenance lineage, and validity envelopes;
- declared probability distributions and option-success models;
- robust-option eligibility across a declared credible-state set;
- option-specific trust, authority, safety, and evidence gates;
- expected utility and next-best-evidence calculations in a declared utility space;
- requalification evidence mapping;
- recovery timeline and Time-to-Trust metrics;
- synthetic benchmark and stress-matrix acceptance contracts;
- source-neutral Mission Recovery Chain projections.

## Governed mission responsibilities

The systems and authorities responsible for the mission retain ownership of:

- canonical mission evidence and identity records;
- ownership, legal authority, and operational permission;
- live command and control;
- evidence authentication and source credibility;
- probability calibration for the actual mission context;
- completeness of utility models and mission economics;
- operational safety and flight qualification;
- mission-readiness approval;
- the final consequential decision.

FMA consumes declared inputs from those governed processes and returns inspectable assurance artifacts for decision preparation and requalification review.

## Public release policy

The public profile contains source-neutral contracts, documentation, validation logic, and synthetic or sanitized examples. Mission-specific evidence, protected identities, operational records, and sensitive program context remain in appropriately controlled systems.
