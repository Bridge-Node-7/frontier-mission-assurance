# Limitations

This profile is a bounded public assurance reference.

It does not:

- control or connect to orbital assets;
- establish ownership, legal authority, or operational permission;
- authenticate real mission evidence;
- infer the posterior supplied to the reference policy;
- prove that declared probability models are calibrated;
- prove that recovery-option utility terms are complete or non-overlapping;
- establish statistical independence merely because no shared declared provenance root is visible;
- replace Mission Graph strategic-option or dependency ownership;
- replace Frontier Decision Engine decision preparation;
- record or authorize a consequential human decision;
- certify mission readiness, recoverability, safety, or flight qualification.

Historical or real-world evidence used for private research must remain outside the public
profile unless deliberately sanitized under the repository's public-boundary rules.
