# Profile Contract

## Contract version

**Scientific Discovery Assurance profile contract:** `0.1`  
**Containing FMA release:** `0.8.0`

The profile contract version is independent from the containing FMA release. Breaking profile-level behavior requires a profile-contract version change. Record/schema versions remain authoritative for the individual portable contracts defined by this profile.

## Purpose

Provide source-neutral evidence architecture for machine-assisted and computational discovery while keeping provenance, priority, research boundaries, proof state, specification equivalence, replication, attribution, and accountable decision ownership explicit.

## Architectural class

**Frontier Mission Assurance profile.** The profile extends FMA through bounded discovery records and validation invariants. It does not modify the universal FMA graph ontology or replace the systems that own the underlying research.

## Profile ownership

The profile owns:

- the public schemas and source-neutral examples under this directory;
- Discovery Passport, research-priority, research-boundary, proof-state, replication, attribution, and agent-provenance contract semantics;
- profile-specific validation invariants;
- the public assurance path from candidate discovery to accountable institutional review.

## Retained system ownership

The surrounding research environment retains ownership of:

- canonical papers, code, data, models, notebooks, experiment records, and source material;
- scientific interpretation and validity;
- specification-equivalence judgment requiring qualified review;
- independent replication activity and conclusions;
- intellectual-property, authorship, publication, safety, regulatory, and institutional decisions;
- generic FMA graph and Decision Receipt semantics;
- final consequential decision authority.

## Inputs

- a declared discovery or research claim;
- source and trigger provenance;
- priority and research-boundary evidence where applicable;
- formal proof evidence where applicable;
- specification-equivalence review state;
- replication evidence and unresolved discrepancies;
- attribution chronology and accountable decision ownership.

## Outputs

- validated portable discovery-assurance records;
- explicit unresolved proof, specification, replication, or attribution obligations;
- reviewable provenance and attribution chronology;
- bounded evidence for downstream accountable decision preparation.

## Decision authority

Machine outputs validate declared structure and preserve evidence distinctions. They do not establish scientific truth, specification equivalence, authorship entitlement, publication priority, or authorization for consequential action. Qualified reviewers and accountable institutions retain those judgments.

## Public evaluation scope

The public contract uses source-neutral examples to demonstrate record semantics,
validation behavior, unresolved states, and decision-authority boundaries.
