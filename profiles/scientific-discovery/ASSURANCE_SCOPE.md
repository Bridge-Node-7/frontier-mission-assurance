# Assurance Scope

Scientific Discovery Assurance provides machine-checkable structure for discovery provenance, priority evidence, research-boundary declarations, formal-proof records, replication, attribution chronology, and accountable decision ownership.

## Profile-owned controls

The profile can verify declared structure and selected cross-record invariants for:

- discovery identity and contributor records;
- source and trigger provenance;
- artifact hashes and declared priority evidence;
- research-boundary attestations;
- formal-proof execution records;
- specification-equivalence review state;
- replication records and unresolved discrepancies;
- attribution chronology;
- explicit decision ownership.

## Authority boundary

Qualified scientific, legal, security, engineering, and institutional processes retain responsibility for:

- scientific validity and interpretation;
- credibility and independence of cited sources;
- enforcement of research-data handling controls;
- trusted priority, publication, and intellectual-property determinations;
- completeness of machine-generated provenance;
- independence of replication parties;
- equivalence between a formal specification and the intended scientific claim;
- deployment, safety, publication, and mission decisions.

The profile preserves these distinctions so machine-verifiable evidence can strengthen review without replacing the authorities responsible for the underlying judgment.

## Public evaluation scope

Public examples are source-neutral and demonstrate only the profile's declared
machine-checkable behavior and limitations.
