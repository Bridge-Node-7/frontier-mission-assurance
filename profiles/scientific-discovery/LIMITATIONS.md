# Limitations

Scientific Discovery Assurance is a public reference profile. It establishes machine-checkable structure and selected cross-record invariants; it does not establish the underlying scientific truth.

It does not:

- prove that a provider enforced a declared research-data boundary;
- establish scientific, legal, patent, or publication priority;
- prove that machine-generated provenance is complete;
- prove independence of a replication party;
- prove that cited sources are credible or independent;
- convert formal proof checking into specification validation;
- authorize publication, deployment, safety acceptance, or mission readiness;
- replace qualified scientific, legal, security, or engineering review.

The public repository must contain only synthetic/public-safe examples. Real research evidence belongs in an appropriately authorized environment.
