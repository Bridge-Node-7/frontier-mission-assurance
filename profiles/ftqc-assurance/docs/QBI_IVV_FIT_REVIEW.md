# QBI IV&V Fit Review

**Review date:** 2026-09-17  
**Basis:** publicly available DARPA QBI program and opportunity materials current at the review date.

This is an independent public-source fit review. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by DARPA, QBI, or any participant.

## Public program signal

Public QBI materials establish several relevant facts:

- QBI evaluates whether quantum-computing approaches can plausibly reach utility-scale operation on the program horizon.
- Stage A focuses on the system concept and evidence supporting feasibility.
- Stage B focuses on the R&D plan, major risks, and risk-reduction prototypes.
- Stage C applies government verification and validation to whether the proposed system can be constructed as designed and operated as intended.
- DARPA separately solicits innovative infrastructure, equipment, and expertise to augment QBI independent verification and validation.
- Public QBI materials distinguish performer roles from independent-evaluation roles and identify potential organizational-conflict considerations.

## Current BN7 fit

### Credible contribution today

Bridge Node 7 can credibly contribute to:

- claim / assumption / evidence structuring;
- provenance and evidence-state separation;
- dependency and change-impact mapping;
- evidence-applicability boundaries;
- expert-review gates;
- decision-basis continuity;
- industrial / supplier / interface dependency visibility;
- source-neutral assurance-context exports;
- Mission Decision Packets and explicit reopen conditions.

### Not established today

Bridge Node 7 does not currently claim independent authority to adjudicate:

- quantum error-correction theory or decoder validity;
- neutral-atom or other qubit-modality physics;
- hardware fidelity, loss, calibration, or metrology;
- FTQC resource-estimator correctness for a real program;
- prototype performance;
- utility-scale system performance;
- government evaluator eligibility or independence.

Those functions require qualified technical experts, test infrastructure, authorized evidence, and the applicable government process.

## Strategic fit

The strongest present positioning is:

> **verification-readiness and evidence-to-decision infrastructure that can support rigorous third-party review**

—not—

> **full-stack quantum IV&V provider**

A future QBI-facing contribution should therefore be evaluated as a bounded component within a qualified team unless Bridge Node 7 develops the missing domain, test, infrastructure, and independence capabilities.

## Team capabilities to add

The highest-priority expert and infrastructure relationships are:

- QEC theory / decoding;
- neutral-atom or relevant modality physics;
- experimental quantum hardware;
- photonics / optical control where applicable;
- controls and real-time systems;
- metrology and calibration;
- systems engineering / test and evaluation;
- manufacturing and industrialization;
- government contracting / organizational-conflict counsel.

## Go / no-go gate for a real IV&V submission

Do not submit merely because the opportunity is adjacent.

Proceed only if a specific contribution can be stated in one sentence and supported by:

1. a real technical or assurance capability;
2. evidence that it adds value beyond the existing evaluator stack;
3. named qualified personnel or partners;
4. the necessary infrastructure or authorized access;
5. an independence / conflict disposition;
6. a bounded work package with measurable acceptance criteria.

Otherwise, continue building public verification-readiness evidence and customer proof.

## Relationship to FTQC Assurance

FTQC Assurance should remain architecture-neutral and useful whether or not a customer participates in QBI.

QBI is an external rigor reference and market signal, not the identity of the profile.
