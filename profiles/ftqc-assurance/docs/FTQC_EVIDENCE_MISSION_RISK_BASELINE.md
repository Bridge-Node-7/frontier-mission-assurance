# FTQC Evidence & Mission-Risk Baseline

The FTQC Evidence & Mission-Risk Baseline is the smallest bounded engagement pattern for making a fault-tolerant quantum computing decision basis verification-ready.

It is not a quantum-computer certification, physics-validation service, government-readiness determination, or substitute for qualified domain review.

## Mission question

> **What must be true for this technical decision to remain defensible, what currently supports those conditions, where does uncertainty remain, and what should be verified next?**

## Minimum baseline

A complete baseline contains:

1. **Decision statement** — one consequential decision and accountable human owner.
2. **Critical claims register** — only the claims that materially control the decision.
3. **Assumption register** — unresolved premises the decision currently depends on.
4. **Evidence map** — authoritative evidence references connected to each critical claim.
5. **Evidence validity envelopes** — where each evidence item applies and what establishes that applicability.
6. **Dependency map** — technical, interface, partner, supplier, manufacturing, control, and schedule dependencies relevant to the decision.
7. **Resource-estimate state** — reported or reproduced estimates with assumptions, configuration identity, limitations, and applicability kept distinct.
8. **Expert review gates** — questions requiring qualified QEC, physics, photonics, controls, metrology, systems, manufacturing, or other domain judgment.
9. **Risk-retirement actions** — the smallest next evidence-producing actions that materially reduce uncertainty.
10. **Decision Receipt / Mission Decision Packet** — current disposition, basis, exclusions, and explicit reopen conditions.

## Expected output

The baseline should allow a reviewer to answer:

- What must be true?
- What is directly supported?
- What remains assumed?
- What evidence is only reported rather than reproduced or independently challenged?
- Which evidence has bounded applicability?
- Which dependencies can invalidate the current basis?
- Where is qualified expert adjudication mandatory?
- What next test, analysis, prototype, or review retires the most decision-relevant uncertainty?
- What decision can an accountable human justify now?
- What change forces reconsideration?

## Domain-expert gate

When a conclusion requires specialist technical authority beyond the bounded FMA contract, mark it:

> **DOMAIN EXPERT REVIEW REQUIRED**

The profile must preserve the unresolved state until an appropriately qualified review is supplied under the applicable process.

## Industrial assurance view

The baseline may include industrial dependencies when they materially control the technical decision:

- critical component availability;
- supplier qualification state;
- manufacturing scale assumptions;
- partner or subsystem interfaces;
- calibration or metrology dependencies;
- long-lead infrastructure;
- single-point dependencies;
- evidence or test capacity.

The presence of an industrial dependency does not establish supplier or manufacturing qualification.

## Success criterion

The baseline succeeds when it reduces the effort required for an independent reviewer, technical leader, investor, acquirer, or government evaluator to reconstruct the decision basis without granting Bridge Node 7 authority it does not possess.

The intended outcome is **verification readiness**, not a favorable verdict.
