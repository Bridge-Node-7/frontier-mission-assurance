# Cross-Domain Epistemic Assurance

Frontier Mission Assurance treats epistemic state as an assurance concern whenever a consequential decision depends on what is known, how it is known, and whether the evidence still applies.

This doctrine does not create a new evidence authority, universal truth schema, certification layer, or decision authority. Domain profiles continue to own their declared evidence semantics. The purpose is to preserve one cross-domain invariant: information must not silently gain a stronger epistemic status as it moves through analysis, automation, assurance, or decision preparation.

## Core invariant

A report, observation, measurement, calculation, inference, simulation, hypothesis, review, and decision are different things.

A downstream projection may summarize them, but it must not collapse those distinctions.

```text
reported
!= observed
!= measured
!= calculated
!= inferred
!= simulated
!= hypothesized
!= reproduced
!= independently challenged
!= qualified
!= authorized
```

Movement between states requires evidence and review appropriate to the domain.

## No authority escalation

Representation inside FMA does not increase factual, scientific, qualification, operational, publication, investment, legal, or mission authority.

A machine PASS establishes only the controls exercised by that validator.

A successful reproduction establishes reproduction under declared conditions. It does not automatically establish scientific truth or applicability elsewhere.

A profile assessment may prepare a decision basis. It does not make the consequential decision.

## Evidence-class preservation

Profiles may use different evidence classes, but the following distinctions should remain inspectable when relevant:

- direct observation;
- instrumented measurement;
- reported testimony or external assertion;
- calculation or derived quantity;
- inference;
- simulation or modeled result;
- independently reproduced result;
- expert judgment;
- hypothesis or interpretation.

Profiles should not invent a universal conversion table among these classes. Admissibility remains governed by the applicable domain contract and qualified review process.

## Promotion rule

Analysis may generate explanations, hypotheses, candidate tests, or next evidence requests.

Promotion to a stronger evidence state requires admissible evidence under the declared policy.

AI generation, repetition, source prestige, narrative coherence, or analyst confidence are not promotion mechanisms.

## Applicability

Evidence remains bounded to the conditions that support its use.

A result may be valid in one configuration while remaining unknown in another.

Where material, record:

- configuration identity;
- time or version;
- operating conditions;
- scale;
- model assumptions;
- calibration state;
- reproduction state;
- expert-review boundary;
- known exclusions.

Loss of applicability should trigger reassessment rather than silent reuse.

## Contradiction

Contradictory evidence must remain visible.

A later finding may supersede a prior basis only through an explicit governed update. Historical decision-time state remains preserved.

Contradiction is not automatically failure. It is a signal that the decision basis may require stronger evidence, narrower scope, or renewed expert judgment.

## AI boundary

AI may assist with extraction, comparison, hypothesis generation, anomaly detection, drafting, or decision preparation.

AI output begins as generated or inferred information unless independently supported.

A model may not promote its own output into canonical evidence, qualification, mission readiness, publication authority, or consequential decision authority.

## Public-boundary rule

Public projections must preserve the same epistemic distinctions that exist in the governed basis.

Publication must not:

- remove attribution;
- convert inference into observation;
- convert reported evidence into independent verification;
- hide material contradictions;
- remove applicability limits;
- strengthen stale or unknown claims through wording alone.

Public release remains separately authorized.

## Relationship to profiles

Orbital Recovery Assurance already applies a profile-specific Epistemic Firewall.

Scientific Discovery Assurance preserves candidate claim, proof, replication, and unresolved-obligation state.

FTQC Assurance preserves reported versus reproduced resource estimates, evidence applicability, expert-review gates, and changed-assumption impact.

Future profiles should apply this doctrine without redefining the assurance core unless repeated real use proves a missing universal contract.

## Adoption rule

Use the smallest domain-appropriate implementation that preserves the invariant.

Do not create a new evidence ontology merely to make different domains look uniform.

Prefer explicit local semantics, governed adapters, and visible limitations over false cross-domain equivalence.

## Permanent principle

> Preserve what happened, what was observed, what was inferred, what was tested, and what a human decided as distinct records of reality.

FMA becomes more trustworthy when it refuses to make uncertain information look more certain than it is.
