# Independence and Review Scope

Frontier Mission Assurance may support different technical-review roles. Any
claim of **independent review** must be limited to the scope for which
independence is actually established.

## Public rule

Do not represent advisory, implementation, evidence-architecture, or other
participatory work as independent validation merely because FMA was used.

When independence matters, the review should make the following clear:

- the bounded subject of review;
- the reviewer's role;
- the evidence basis and material exclusions;
- the authority under which the review is performed; and
- any limitations that affect how the result may be interpreted.

If independence cannot be established for the claimed scope, describe the work
according to the role actually performed.

## FMA semantics

FMA can preserve evidence, assumptions, dependencies, review state, and human
disposition. It does not by itself create reviewer independence, certification,
regulatory status, government authorization, or decision authority.

Independence requirements remain governed by the applicable engagement,
process, contract, standard, authority, and qualified review function.
