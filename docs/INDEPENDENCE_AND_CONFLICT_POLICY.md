# Independence and Conflict Policy

Frontier Mission Assurance depends on trustworthy boundaries between advisory work, technical contribution, and independent evaluation.

> **Independence is institutional capital.**

Bridge Node 7 treats independence as a capability that can be strengthened, constrained, or impaired by the roles it accepts. Revenue does not convert advisory work into independent verification and validation, and a prior contribution to a design must not later be represented as independent review of that same contribution.

This document is an institutional operating policy. It is not a legal opinion, a government organizational-conflict-of-interest determination, or a substitute for the rules of an applicable solicitation, contract, standard, regulator, or qualified counsel.

## Engagement classes

Every consequential assurance engagement is classified before work begins.

| Class | Typical role | Independence representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public research | Source-neutral research, public crosswalks, public reference cases | No client or evaluator independence claim |
| Performer-side assurance | Evidence architecture, verification-readiness, decision-basis continuity, risk-retirement support | Advisory / assurance support; not independent V&V unless separately established |
| Buyer-side or diligence support | Technical diligence, evidence review, acquisition or investment decision support | Independence bounded to the disclosed scope and relationships |
| Governed independent evaluation | Explicit IV&V or other formally independent evaluator role | Independence exists only under the governing process and accepted conflict controls |

Role labels must follow the actual engagement. They must not be upgraded by marketing language.

## Pre-engagement conflict screen

Before accepting a consequential engagement, record whether the proposed role creates or may create:

- access to proprietary, controlled, or otherwise nonpublic information;
- participation in architecture, design, implementation, test planning, or acceptance criteria;
- advocacy, representation, or success-fee obligations;
- future review of work that Bridge Node 7 or a retained expert helped create;
- overlapping work for organizations whose interests may conflict;
- financial dependence that could reasonably impair objective judgment;
- actual, potential, or apparent conflicts under a relevant customer or government process;
- restrictions created by nondisclosure, intellectual-property, export-control, security, or data-handling obligations;
- expert, subcontractor, laboratory, or partner relationships that affect the claimed independence of a review.

The screen is evidence for role selection. It is not itself a legal OCI determination.

## Dispositions

A conflict screen ends in one of four dispositions:

- **ACCEPT** — no material conflict identified within the declared role.
- **ACCEPT WITH SAFEGUARDS** — proceed only with named controls.
- **EXTERNAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — obtain qualified contracts, legal, security, ethics, or program review before proceeding.
- **DECLINE / RE-SCOPE** — do not accept the role as proposed.

The disposition, rationale, reviewer, date, and reopen conditions should be versioned with the governed engagement record.

## Safeguards

Depending on the engagement, safeguards may include:

- narrower scope and explicit non-claims;
- separation of advisory and evaluator personnel;
- information barriers and separate governed workspaces;
- disclosure of relevant prior work and financial relationships;
- recusal from reviewing prior BN7-authored design decisions;
- independent specialist or laboratory review;
- customer acknowledgement of the declared role;
- external legal or government-contracting review;
- declining work whose conflict cannot be credibly controlled.

No safeguard may be used to relabel self-review as independent V&V.

## Self-review rule

A person or organization that materially designed, implemented, selected, or advocated for a technical approach must not represent its later review of that same contribution as independent V&V merely because the review uses a separate document, team label, or contract line item.

If prior involvement is relevant, disclose it and use the correct review class.

## Government and formal IV&V boundary

For government, regulated, acquisition, or formally independent evaluator roles:

1. the current solicitation, contract, statement of work, program guidance, and applicable law control;
2. required disclosures and representations must be made through the authorized process;
3. program-specific independence and conflict determinations remain with the authorized customer and qualified counsel;
4. the public FMA repository must not be treated as evidence of eligibility for a particular evaluator role.

The FTQC profile's QBI material remains a public-source learning and interoperability surface. It does not establish QBI eligibility, affiliation, evaluator authority, or readiness.

## Reopen conditions

An accepted independence disposition should reopen when any of the following changes materially:

- scope;
- customer or counterparty;
- personnel or subcontractors;
- proprietary information access;
- design participation;
- financial relationship;
- intended downstream evaluator role;
- solicitation or governing requirement;
- relevant organizational relationship.

## Public/private boundary

This public policy contains the reusable institutional rule set. Actual conflict screens, customer identities, contractual restrictions, proprietary relationships, legal analyses, and program-specific determinations belong in governed records outside the public repository.

## Institutional test

Before accepting work, ask:

> **Could Bridge Node 7 later explain, precisely and credibly, what role it played, what it did not do, which evidence it saw, and why any claimed independence is legitimate?**

If that answer cannot be documented, the role should be narrowed, externally reviewed, or declined.
